Study posts are grouped into categories in the sidebar, chosen automatically by
Claude based on each video's content. New categories appear here as soon as the
first post in that topic is published.

Don't see a video you want studied? [Open a request](https://github.com/jeonck/playlist/issues/new?template=youtube-request.yml)
with the YouTube link — once a maintainer approves it, a post appears here automatically.
